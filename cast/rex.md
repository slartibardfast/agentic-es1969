# Rex — the Vintage-Hardware Listener

*Runs a real ES1969 card on a modern PC for its native ESFM synth.*

**Modality: embodied, occasional, hardware-first.** Rex keeps an ESS Solo-1 PCI
card in a current desktop because its on-chip ESFM synthesizer voices old MIDI and
DOS-era game music the way no software emulator quite matches. He installs an OS
maybe once a year, reads forum threads more often than documentation, and judges
success by ear.

- **Goals:** get the card recognised and playing in native ESFM mode on Windows 10
  or 11 x64; keep MPU-401 MIDI working; avoid bricking a working machine.
- **Frustrations:** no signed 64-bit driver exists, so the card shows an
  exclamation mark in Device Manager; Secure Boot and driver-signature enforcement
  block test-signed drivers; instructions assume hardware he no longer has.
- **Works by:** following a written install procedure step by step, rebooting when
  told to, and reporting back when a step does not match what he sees on screen.
