# Cast — the project's Who

Personas: hypothetical archetypal actors, drawn from research, used to keep the
work grounded in who it serves (Powell, Keenan & McDaid 2007, after Cooper &
Reimann's Interaction Design).

The software under development is a reconstruction of the ESS Technology ES1969
(Solo-1) audio driver, plus a generic MPU-401 gameport driver, for modern 64-bit
Windows. Its cast is the people who run that hardware and the people who keep the
driver alive:

- [rex.md](rex.md) — the vintage-hardware listener who wants native ESFM MIDI on
  a current 64-bit Windows machine.
- [morgan.md](morgan.md) — the retro-PC builder running era-correct Windows
  98SE/ME and Windows 2000/XP rigs.
- [dana.md](dana.md) — the driver maintainer continuing the reconstruction.

Build each new persona by discussion, eliciting the actual users of the driver.
See [applying-personas.md](applying-personas.md) for the process.
